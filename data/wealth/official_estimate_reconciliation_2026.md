# February/May official-estimate comparison — 20 September 2026

## Result: bounded evidence gap, not a numerical reconciliation

The reviewed open sources do **not establish why the 10→14m threshold contribution is −730m in February and −830m in May**. Different stated comparison baselines are verified; their quantitative contribution to the difference is not. Do not describe the difference as a demonstrated model revision, subtract incompatible headline figures to explain it, or calibrate the dashboard to either figure.

Two additional findings narrow the gap:

- The **original May proposition**, chapter 3, printed p. 10, already states −830m accrued, +550m against adoption assumptions, and −280m for the package. Neither linked correction letter changes these chapter-3 estimates. The 11 June corrected-edition label is therefore **not the origin of 830m**.
- The **27 February published speech manuscript** explicitly describes −730m as compared with 2025 and reports a −280m package total. It is marked “Ikke sjekket mot fremføring”; this is a published manuscript, not a verified transcript. Matching package totals in February and May do not establish identical counterfactuals or a numerical bridge.

## What is explained, and what is not

1. **January versus earlier model estimates:** SSB explicitly explains that its earlier +423m estimate used 2020 data projected to 2024, while the new estimate uses 2023 records and new 2024 housing values projected to 2026. Different years and data are stated reasons. This is a published explanation for that comparison, **not for February −730 versus May −830**.
2. **January correction:** SSB's note says the category counts and corresponding category changes were corrected; the total change was unchanged. It cannot explain a later 100m threshold difference.
3. **May internal accounting:** Chapter 3 itself gives +550, −830 and combined −280. These belong to its own comparison framework. They are not three threshold reforms or an explanation of the February number.
4. **February package:** Slide 5 gives +1,560 / −580 / −730 / −525. The manuscript states a rounded −280 package outcome. Do not treat small rounding differences or subtract the January +973 from these bars as evidence of revisions.
5. **Hypotheses only:** counterfactual parameter choices, ordering/allocation of interacting policy effects, refreshed data/projection factors or an unreported correction could matter. None is quantified or identified as the cause of the 100m difference in the reviewed material. The stated baseline difference alone does not prove causation.

A defensible bridge would require a published explanation or matched calculations documenting both counterfactual tax schedules, the same population and valuation inputs, and any changed data/interaction treatment. No such bridge was found within this search. This does not prove none exists elsewhere.
